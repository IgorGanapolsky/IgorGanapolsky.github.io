Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before installing

How does Dayglass record AirPods calls without a bot?

Dayglass uses native macOS ScreenCaptureKit to record both your microphone and headphone/system audio directly on Apple Silicon. The background capture daemon uses ~93 MB idle RAM (local Whisper and LLM inference run only when transcribing or summarizing). No cloud bot ever joins your Zoom, Teams, or Meet call.

Does any screen or audio data leave my Mac?

Never. All Apple Vision OCR frames, audio transcripts, and meeting notes live in ~/.dayglass/dayglass.sqlite on your local disk. Password managers, banking tabs, and private windows are automatically skipped.

Why choose Dayglass over Granola or MacWhisper?

Granola is great for bot-free meeting notes, and MacWhisper is great for transcribing audio files. Dayglass combines 100% local bot-free meeting notes with searchable screen OCR (slides, docs, terminal errors) and a built-in MCP server so Claude Code and Cursor automatically know what was decided in your meetings.

🔒 Privacy Policy & Participant Consent: Dayglass stores 100% of your screen frames, audio recordings, transcripts, and meeting notes locally on your Mac under ~/.dayglass and never uploads your content to our servers. Use the built-in 1-Click Call Consent Snippet to notify call participants and always follow applicable call-recording consent laws in your jurisdiction. Questions or security audits?
