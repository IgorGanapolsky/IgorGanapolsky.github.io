What it is

ApplyOps is the application-submission layer I built and operate at production scale. It drives a real Google Chrome session (via Patchright CDP-attach) through Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, LinkedIn Easy Apply, Dice, and Alignerr — each with its own driver that knows the gotchas of that ATS.

Why real Chrome? Because reCAPTCHA Enterprise v3 score-rejects bundled Chromium. Because Workday's React inputs hate cua-driver's CGEvent focus routing. Because Greenhouse silently rejects the bundled-browser fingerprint at the gate. Real Chrome with my cf_clearance + JA3 passes those gates.
