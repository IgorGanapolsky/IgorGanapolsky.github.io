What gets audited

The diagnostic focuses on operational workflows that repeat every week across active Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, and direct-booking properties.

Guest messages

Wi-Fi, parking, check-in, late checkout, house rules, local recommendations, complaint tone, and escalation triggers.

Maintenance triage

Detect urgency, route cleaner or maintenance tasks, separate cosmetic problems from safety or access issues, and log follow-up.

Owner operations

Daily exceptions, open issues, unresolved guest concerns, and the metrics an operator needs before deciding what to automate.

The guardrail table

Claude should not invent policy, promise refunds, expose access details, or continue when confidence is low.
