Partner / acquisition brief

Resume-risk audits for technical candidates before writing or submission.

ApplyOps helps resume-writing, staffing, and executive-search teams identify unsupported claims, ATS readability issues, title-fit gaps, and weak bullets before a candidate is rewritten, coached, or submitted.

Start a partner conversation View sample audit

Best fit

  • Resume firms serving senior technical or executive candidates.
  • Staffing teams that want cleaner candidate submissions.
  • Career coaches who need a fast diagnostic before paid strategy work.
  • Executive-search teams that want defensible candidate positioning.

What the workflow checks

The product is built around recruiter trust, not template polish.

Claim defensibility

Flags metrics, scope claims, AI claims, and leadership language that may be hard to defend.

ATS readability

Checks whether titles, dates, company names, skills, and bullets can be parsed cleanly.

Target-title fit

Compares the resume's strongest evidence against the role family the candidate wants next.
