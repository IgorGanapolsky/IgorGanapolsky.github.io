Problem

Hilltown needed a payment architecture for a venue ticketing workflow where the venue remains the payment/tax boundary, while the platform can still collect its fee without contaminating venue sales-tax math.

The work had to fit a Pretix plugin environment, avoid brittle floating point money math, and preserve a clean route for refunds, platform fees, and later on-site monetization.

Architecture delivered

  • Stripe Connect destination-charge flow for marketplace-style payments.
  • Platform-fee isolation so the platform fee remains decoupled from venue tax liability.
  • Money conversion using decimal rounding discipline instead of float math.
  • Pretix plugin boundaries around payment, signals, and tests.
  • Escrow-first delivery controls: funded milestone before implementation work.
