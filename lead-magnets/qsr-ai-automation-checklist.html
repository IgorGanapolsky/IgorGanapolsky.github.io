QSR AI Automation Audit Checklist

Use this before buying AI phone ordering, workflow automation, or a new restaurant tech stack.

  1. Count missed calls and abandoned inquiries for one week.
  2. Capture three staff handoff failures.
  3. List every place an order is copied manually.
  4. Identify one repeatable message that can be automated.
  5. Estimate the value of one recovered order, one saved labor hour, or one recovered catering lead.
  6. Pick one workflow: missed-call capture, catering follow-up, kitchen handoff, or review request.
  7. Decide whether the fix is a checklist, n8n workflow, voice agent, or full diagnostic.
