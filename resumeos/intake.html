Send this intake packet

  • Your full name and best email address.
  • Your LinkedIn URL.
  • Your current resume link (Dropbox, Google Drive, or similar). Make sure link permissions are set to public view.
  • One target role family (e.g. Staff Android Engineer or AI Platform Engineer).
  • Three to five job links that represent the roles you want.
  • Constraints: location, remote/hybrid preference, compensation floor, visa sponsorship, and availability.

Do not send SSN, date of birth, passport numbers, bank details, or other high-risk personal data.
