Send this intake packet

  • Your full name and best email address.
  • Your LinkedIn URL.
  • Your current resume link. Use a Google Drive or Dropbox link that is viewable.
  • One target role family, for example Staff Android Engineer or AI Platform Engineer.
  • Three to five job links that represent the roles you want.
  • Constraints: location, remote/hybrid, compensation floor, sponsorship, and availability.

Do not send SSN, date of birth, passport numbers, bank details, or other high-risk personal data.
