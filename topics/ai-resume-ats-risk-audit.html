AI-generated resumes often look polished while creating new risk: oversized claims, generic platform language, repeated buzzwords, and bullets that are hard to defend.

What to check before applying

  • Does every AI, cloud, or platform claim point to real shipped work?
  • Are role titles and dates readable without layout tricks?
  • Are skills tied to outcomes, not just keyword lists?
  • Would a hiring manager believe the scope from the resume alone?

Why this matters

Recruiters are filtering more aggressively. A resume that sounds too broad or too automated can lose trust before the candidate gets a call.
